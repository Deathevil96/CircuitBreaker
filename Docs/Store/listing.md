## Full description (max 4000)

Arrow Breaker is a quiet logic puzzle about untangling arrows.

Every arrow points the way it will travel. Tap an arrow with a clear path and it slides out along its own line. Tap one that is blocked and it bumps back. Work out which arrow has to leave first, then the next, until the whole board is clear.

The first levels are small rectangles. From level 21 the boards take shape: a heart, a cat, a fish, a person, a rocket, a butterfly and more than twenty others. Only the edge of the shape stays lit. The rest of the grid appears when an arrow crosses it, so the picture reveals itself as you clear it.

PLAY YOUR WAY
- Levels: an endless ladder from Easy to Nightmare. Every level can be solved, with no guessing.
- Daily Run: a new board every day, the same for everyone. Clear every day of a month for the monthly badge.
- Freeplay: pick a difficulty and play as many fresh boards as you like.
- Overload: a score attack against a clock that speeds up.

SIX DIFFICULTIES
Easy, Medium, Hard, Super Hard, Ultra Hard and Nightmare. Hard and harder levels appear along the way with ice, keys and padlocks, bigger boards and better rewards.

TRACK YOUR PROGRESS
Day streaks, win streaks, your best day with the date it happened, and more than sixty achievements, including first-attempt awards and Nightmare clears.

DESIGNED TO STAY OUT OF THE WAY
- Minimal interface, one thin neon line per arrow
- Leave a level and continue exactly where you left it
- Works offline
- No forced ads. Watch an optional ad for an extra hint. A banner sits at the bottom, clear of the board.

Free to play. Contains ads.
