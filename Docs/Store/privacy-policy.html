Privacy Policy for Arrow Breaker

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Arrow Breaker ("the app") is a puzzle game published by Misal Interactive ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and the choices you have. By using the app you agree to this policy and to our Terms and Conditions.

What stays on your device

Your progress (levels, coins, items, purchases made, statistics, achievements and settings) is saved on your device. We do not run our own accounts or servers and we do not receive this data. Uninstalling the app, clearing its data, or resetting your device deletes it, and we cannot restore it.

In-app purchases

The app lets you buy virtual coins and an ad-free option through Google Play Billing. Payments are processed entirely by Google. We never see or store your card number, bank details or Google account password. After a purchase, Google Play tells the app that it succeeded, together with the product bought and an order identifier. The app uses this to give you your purchase, to restore it if you reinstall, and to keep your coin balance consistent. This purchase record is kept on your device. If you contact us about a purchase, we may ask for your Google Play order number, which we use only to look into your request.

Purchases are not offered in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland. To decide whether to show the shop, the app reads your device's region setting (such as the mobile network or system region). This check happens on your device and the result is not sent to us.

Refunds are covered in the Terms and Conditions and by Google Play's own refund policy.

Google Play Games Services

The app can use Google Play Games Services to sign you in and to record your achievements. Signing in is optional. If you sign in, Google processes your Play Games profile (such as your player ID and display name) and the achievements you unlock, under Google's own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. We do not receive your name or email address. You can sign out, or delete your Play Games data, from the Google Play Games app or your Google account settings.

Advertising

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob, including optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch in return for a benefit. To show and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and use:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information such as model, operating system and diagnostics,
  • how you interact with ads.

In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, we ask for your consent before ads are requested, using Google's consent form. You can change your choice in the app's privacy options when available. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, in your device's Google settings. Learn more at https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads. Buying the ad-free option removes banner and full-screen ads, but not the optional rewarded ads.

Crash and performance data

Google Play may provide us with anonymous crash and performance reports through Android vitals.

Who receives information

The only third parties that handle information through the app are Google (Google Play, Google Play Billing, Google Play Games Services and Google AdMob) and the advertising partners Google works with. We do not sell personal information and we do not share it for other purposes. We may disclose information we hold if the law requires it, which in practice is limited to a purchase order number you give us.

What we do not do

We do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos, microphone, or precise location. We do not include analytics or social features of our own, and we do not build profiles of you.

How long information is kept

Data on your device stays until you remove it. Messages you send us are kept for as long as needed to answer you and for a reasonable period afterwards for record keeping. Information held by Google is kept under Google's policies.

Your rights

Because we hold almost no personal information about you, there is usually nothing for us to access, correct or delete. Depending on where you live (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information, to object to processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. For ad-related data, use Google's controls above. For anything else, email us and we will respond within a reasonable time and in line with the law that applies to you. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Where the law requires a legal basis: we show personalised ads only with your consent where consent is required; we handle purchase records to perform our contract with you; and we answer your messages for our legitimate interest in supporting players.

International transfers and security

Google processes data in many countries. We keep what little we hold on your device and use the protections provided by Android and Google Play. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 (or under the higher age of digital consent in your country), and we do not knowingly collect information from them. Purchases are intended for adults, or for minors with a parent or guardian's permission. If you believe a child has given us information, email us and we will delete it.

Changes and contact
