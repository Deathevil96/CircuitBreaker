What's new in 1.0.3 (Google Play, max 500 characters)

- New, trickier levels: arrows now fill the whole board and curl round each other
- Dots mark the path every arrow takes as it leaves
- A new celebration when you clear a level, with encouraging words in many languages
- Free coins button on the main menu shows its amount and countdown again
- Achievements are now just for bragging rights, no coin rewards
- Bug fixes and polish
